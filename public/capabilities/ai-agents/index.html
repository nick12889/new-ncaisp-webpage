BEYOND A CONVERSATION

An agent can do more
than answer a question.

The actions available to an agent depend on its tools, permissions and the approval rules you agree.

01

Understand

Interpret the task and relevant context.

02

Reason

Select an appropriate next step within scope.

03

Retrieve

Find approved information and sources.

04

Use tools

Interact with authorised applications and APIs.

05

Act

Carry out permitted tasks and record results.

06

Communicate

Explain results and request missing information.

07

Coordinate

Pass work between agents and business systems.

08

Escalate

Ask a person when authority or certainty is insufficient.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What is a custom AI agent?

A specialised agent that retrieves information, reasons within a defined scope, uses tools and takes permitted actions. Each agent has a clear purpose and a route back to your team.

What kinds of AI agents can NCAISP build?

Sales and customer service agents, operations and finance agents, research and knowledge agents, voice and scheduling agents, and document analysis and support agents.

Can an AI agent take actions without human approval?

Each agent works with approved knowledge, tools and permissions, and includes human escalation. Important actions can be set to require explicit human approval.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What is retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)?

RAG is an approach where an AI application first retrieves relevant information from approved sources, such as your documents or knowledge base, and then uses it to generate an answer.

How do you choose the right language model?

The approach is selected against your data, quality, latency and cost needs. Model routing and orchestration can use different models for different tasks.

A purpose-built application with a model strategy, knowledge connections and repeatable quality checks.

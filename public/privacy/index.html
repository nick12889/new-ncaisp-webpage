PRIVACY NOTICE

How we handle your information.

What this website collects, why, who processes it and the choices you have.

Last updated 17 October 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by NCAI Strategy Partners (registered business name: NC Strategy Ops), based in Dublin, Ireland, which is responsible for the personal information described here. Contact us about privacy at hello@ncaistrategypartners.com.

The chat assistant (Aileen)

Aileen is an AI assistant, not a person, and can make mistakes. When you chat, your messages and the recent conversation are sent through our server to Cloudflare Workers AI to generate replies. Aileen asks for your name, business email address and phone number, and you may also mention your company. Please do not share sensitive or confidential information in the chat.

When the chat ends, or when you close the page, we:

  • save the conversation transcript, an AI-generated summary and your contact details in our database (Cloudflare D1);
  • email you a copy of the summary, and email the summary and transcript to our team, using Resend;
  • add your name, email address, phone number, company and the chat summary to our customer relationship management (CRM) system, Twenty.

We use this information to answer your questions, follow up on your enquiry and prepare for any conversation you ask for. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, and taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. Aileen does not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Voice input and spoken replies

If you choose to use the microphone, your browser asks for permission first. The recording of your question is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to be turned into text and is not stored by us; the resulting text is treated as a chat message. Spoken replies are produced by Cloudflare Workers AI from Aileen’s written answers.

Booking a strategy session

The booking calendar is provided by Cal.com and loads when you reach the booking section. Information you enter when booking is processed by Cal.com under its own terms and privacy policy, and shared with us so we can hold the meeting.

Browser storage and technical data

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. The day/night preference on the homepage is saved in your browser’s local storage and is not sent to us. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical information such as your IP address and browser details to deliver and protect the website.

Who processes your information

  • Cloudflare: website hosting, security, AI processing (chat replies, speech-to-text and text-to-speech) and our database.
  • Resend: sending chat summary emails.
  • Twenty: our CRM system.
  • Cal.com: appointment booking.

Some of these providers process data outside Ireland, the UK and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards the law requires, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep chat transcripts, summaries and contact details for up to 24 months after the chat, then delete them from our database automatically. If you become a client, we keep the records we need for the engagement and our legal obligations. Emails and CRM records are deleted on the same schedule, or sooner on request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct or delete your information, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive a copy in a portable format. Email hello@ncaistrategypartners.com and we will respond within one month. If you are in Ireland or the EU, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie). In the UK, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk). We would appreciate the chance to resolve any concern first.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when our services or providers change. The date at the top shows the latest version.

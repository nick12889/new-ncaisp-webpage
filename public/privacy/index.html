Privacy in this preview.

This site is a private review version of the NCAI Strategy Partners website. Registered business name: NC Strategy Ops.

What this website does

The site presents service information and interactive diagrams. It includes a Cal.com booking calendar and a chat panel. It does not access your microphone or include marketing analytics. The interactive diagrams change only the page view. The homepage day/night preference is saved in your browser’s local storage and is not sent to us. The embedded booking calendar is provided by Cal.com and loads its service when you reach the booking section. Appointment information is processed by Cal.com under its terms. The website guide uses preset answers. If live AI is enabled, the panel identifies it before you send a message; your question and recent conversation are then sent through our server to Cloudflare Workers AI to generate a reply. Avoid sharing sensitive or confidential information. Chat history stays in page memory and can be cleared; we do not save a conversation database.

Hosting and access

The hosting platform handles access to this private preview and may process technical request and access information under its own terms. This page does not replace those platform terms.

Before public launch

The public website will need a privacy notice that identifies the legal operator, contact route, actual data flows, providers, retention periods and applicable visitor rights. Any booking, communications or analytics services must be reflected in that notice when connected.

ILLUSTRATIVE USE CASES — NOT CLIENT CASE STUDIES

What could an implementation look like?

Management

ILLUSTRATIVE USE CASE 01

Management reporting brief

Business problem
Leaders assemble updates from spreadsheets, reports and departmental emails.
Proposed workflow
Gather approved updates, reconcile source dates and draft a source-linked management brief with unresolved questions.
Human oversight
Department owners validate the inputs; management approves decisions.
What to measure
Preparation time, source coverage and correction rate.

ILLUSTRATIVE USE CASE 02

Decision and action follow-through

Business problem
Meeting decisions lose ownership between teams.
Proposed workflow
Extract proposed actions from approved meeting notes, suggest owners and dates, then track confirmed tasks in the existing project system.
Human oversight
Attendees confirm actions before assignment; sensitive notes retain access restrictions.
What to measure
Unassigned actions, overdue tasks and time spent preparing follow-up.

ILLUSTRATIVE USE CASE 03

Strategic scenario research

Business problem
Strategy teams spend time collecting evidence for planning assumptions.
Proposed workflow
Organise approved internal data and cited external research into comparison briefs with explicit assumptions and open questions.
Human oversight
Leaders review evidence and assumptions; scenarios are not forecasts or guarantees.
What to measure
Research turnaround, citation coverage and assumptions validated.

Operations

ILLUSTRATIVE USE CASE 01

Customer onboarding coordination

Business problem
New clients require repeated handovers across forms, documents and systems.
Proposed workflow
Check required information, prepare records and route missing items to the responsible team through an onboarding workflow.
Human oversight
A person approves exceptions and activation; access follows the client record.
What to measure
Onboarding time, missing information and handover failures.

ILLUSTRATIVE USE CASE 02

Internal request routing

Business problem
Service requests arrive in multiple inboxes with unclear ownership.
Proposed workflow
Classify requests, retrieve the relevant procedure and suggest routing to the appropriate queue.
Human oversight
Staff confirm unusual or consequential requests; escalation rules remain explicit.
What to measure
Routing accuracy, queue time and reassignment rate.

ILLUSTRATIVE USE CASE 03

Operational exception review

Business problem
Teams manually search reports for stalled jobs and inconsistent records.
Proposed workflow
Compare permitted system records against agreed rules, group exceptions and prepare a daily review queue.
Human oversight
Operations staff validate anomalies and authorise corrective actions.
What to measure
Exception detection time, false positives and resolution time.
